How it works

A retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system: it finds the relevant law first, then has an LLM explain only what it found.

1

Parse the sources

The full Labor Code (317 articles, with footnotes) and DOLE's 2024 Handbook, parsed from the official PDFs with tables and footnotes kept intact.

2

Retrieve

Your question becomes a vector and is compared with 700+ passages. Article numbers are also looked up directly, including pre-2015 ones.

3

Answer with citations

The LLM answers only from the retrieved passages and cites each one. If they don't cover the question, it says so.
